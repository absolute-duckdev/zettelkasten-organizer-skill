# Notes are conversations with a future self

A permanent note is read, above all, by one person: its author, months or
years later, with none of today's context in their head. Writing for that
reader means full sentences, the idea stated plainly, and the *reason it
mattered* recorded — because the future self will not remember why this
seemed important. A note that only makes sense today is a letter with no
address.

## Why it matters

It turns "write clearly" from a stylistic preference into the functional
requirement of the whole system: notes that can't survive the trip across
time are indistinguishable from notes never taken.
