# Ahrens — How to Take Smart Notes (2017)

Only the ideas relevant to my questions, paraphrased — not a summary of
the whole book.

- Writing notes is not the byproduct of reading — it is the actual work
  of understanding. You don't think first and write after; the writing
  *is* the thinking. (ch. 1)
- The slip-box imposes no order upfront. Structure emerges bottom-up from
  clusters of connected notes, which is why it can surprise you. (ch. 2)
- Permanent notes must be written as if for publication: full sentences,
  no assumed context, one idea per note. (ch. 3)

> "Nobody ever starts from scratch." (p. 42)
