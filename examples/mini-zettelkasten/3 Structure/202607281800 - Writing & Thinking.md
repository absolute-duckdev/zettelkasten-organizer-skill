# Writing & Thinking

Entry point into the notes on how writing relates to thinking. The
cluster's open question: does the quality of writing *cause* the quality
of thought, or merely reveal it?

## Core claims

- [[202607281709 - Writing externalizes thought]] — writing forces a
  precision that pure thinking skips; unfinished ideas fail on paper
- [[202607281902 - Notes are conversations with a future self]] — a note
  that only makes sense today is functionally a note never taken
- [[202607281815 - Emergent structure beats imposed taxonomy]] — bottom-up
  order is more robust than top-down filing, because it fits the material
  instead of the designer's assumptions
